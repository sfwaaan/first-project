Starbucks is the world's largest coffeehouse chain, renowned for popularising second-wave coffee culture globally by offering premium espressos, handcrafted cold drinks like Frappuccinos, and a welcoming "third place" environment. Officially founded in Seattle, Washington in 1971, the multinational giant operates over 35,000 stores worldwide. In India, the brand operates as Tata Starbucks, featuring highly localized menu items alongside its iconic global coffee lineup.
